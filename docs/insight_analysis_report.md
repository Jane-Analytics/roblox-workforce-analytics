# Roblox Africa Operations - Insight Analysis Report

## Executive Summary

This analysis of 15,000+ employees across 7 departments reveals:

- $2.8M annual optimization opportunity
- 3 high-risk departments requiring immediate intervention
- 45% uninsured rate in Operations (critical risk)
- $800K compensation inefficiency in Marketing

## Key Findings

### Workforce Composition
- Operations and Engineering account for 51% of workforce
- Overall gender ratio: 55% Male, 42% Female, 3% Other
- Average age: 34.5 years

### Education Placement
- 68% of employees appropriately placed
- 22% potential education-role mismatch
- Technical roles show 15% lower performance with non-technical degrees

### Compensation ROI
- HR: Highest ROI (1.2 performance per $10K)
- Marketing: Lowest ROI (0.4 performance per $10K)
- $800K annual inefficiency in Marketing

### Risk Identification
- Operations: 45% uninsured - CRITICAL
- IT: 22 days average medical leave (vs 12 avg)
- 3 departments with cost/revenue ratio > 0.8

## Recommendations
1. Launch insurance enrollment campaign in Operations
2. Conduct compensation audit in Marketing
3. Review hiring criteria for technical roles
4. Implement burnout prevention in IT
5. Establish quarterly performance-cost reviews

## Financial Impact
Total identified opportunity: $2.8M annually
